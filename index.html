Happy Morse

Customer feedback → evidence-backed product decisions.

Live · Invite-only

Happy Morse helps product teams make sense of scattered customer feedback, identify the problems worth solving and turn approved decisions into delivery work.

The problem

Feedback lives across support conversations, team channels and documents. Recurring problems are difficult to spot, and the reasoning behind a roadmap decision can get lost between discovery and delivery.

My role

I lead the non-engineering side of the startup: product direction, UI/UX, requirements, prioritisation, product communication and launch coordination. I work with engineering to translate that direction into a usable product.

What I own

  • Product definition: framing customer problems, defining scope and shaping the roadmap.
  • UI/UX: information architecture, user journeys, interface design and the experience from onboarding to a decision.
  • Delivery clarity: requirements, acceptance criteria, usability review and feedback for engineering.
  • Startup operations: product positioning, communication and coordination around launch and iteration.

How the product works

  1. 01
    Bring feedback together

    Capture feedback through manual entry, CSV imports and pasted conversations, with integration workflows for connected tools.

  2. 02
    Understand the customer problem

    AI-assisted analysis groups related signals, links them to existing problems or work, and checks matches through independent verification.

  3. 03
    Make a decision with context

    A ranked Decision Inbox surfaces problems to review. Problem briefs bring together customer quotes, impact context, linked work and recommendations.

  4. 04
    Review, approve and deliver

    Keep a record of decisions and their evidence, draft cited product documents, then review proposed handoffs before sending work to delivery tools.

Explore the product’s capabilities
Decision Inbox
A focused list of problems, growing demand, critical reports and decisions that new evidence may warrant revisiting.
Customer Problems & Feedback
Evidence-linked briefs and organised feedback, with corrections for duplicates, misplaced reports and uncertain matches.
Product Backlog
Existing work provides context for new feedback so teams can see whether demand relates to planned or shipped work.
Decision History
Decisions retain evidence snapshots and optional revisit conditions, making the reasoning behind product choices traceable.
Documents & delivery
AI-assisted documents cite evidence, linked work and decisions. Approved work can be reviewed as a ticket plan before external publication.
Connected workflows
The product codebase includes Linear, Jira, Intercom, Slack and Notion integrations. Availability depends on workspace configuration.

Product technology: TypeScript, Next.js / React, PostgreSQL / Drizzle, pgvector, Clerk, Inngest and Vercel AI SDK.

AI supports analysis and drafting; people review decisions and approve external delivery.
